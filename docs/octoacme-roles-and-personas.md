# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide business sponsorship, strategic direction, and high-level oversight for major initiatives. They ensure that the project remains aligned with organizational goals, funding priorities, and measurable value delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the business case and strategic alignment for the initiative
- Sponsor resource decisions and escalation paths for major issues
- Monitor outcome delivery against organizational priorities
- Support stakeholder alignment and policy decisions when trade-offs arise
- Review progress at a high level and remove barriers that require executive attention

### Goals
- Ensure business value and strategic relevance are maintained
- Protect the project from organizational and resourcing risks
- Keep executive stakeholders informed and aligned

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee meetings and executive reviews
- Business case and investment updates
- High-level milestone and risk escalation briefings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to review execution health, risks, and major decisions
- Aligns with the Product Manager on business priorities, value measures, and scope trade-offs
- Supports Developers and cross-functional teams by providing sponsorship and resolving enterprise-level blockers

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architecture Owners guide the system design, technical standards, and implementation quality for the project. They translate business requirements into a feasible technical direction and help the team reduce architectural and integration risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architectural principles, and key design decisions
- Review solution designs for scalability, reliability, and maintainability
- Identify technical dependencies, integration constraints, and platform risks
- Support estimation, implementation planning, and delivery trade-offs
- Ensure alignment between engineering practices and organizational standards

### Goals
- Deliver a robust and maintainable technical solution
- Reduce avoidable architecture and integration risk
- Support the team with clear technical guidance and standards

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency and risk planning with engineering leads
- Design decisions, trade-offs, and implementation guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to shape implementation decisions and standards
- Collaborates with the Project Manager to manage technical dependencies and delivery sequencing
- Works with the Product Manager to ensure technical feasibility is considered in scope and roadmap decisions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect stakeholder needs, project goals, and delivery outcomes. They clarify requirements, ensure measurable business value, and support effective decision-making across planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requirements and document business processes, constraints, and outcomes
- Translate stakeholder needs into clear user stories, acceptance criteria, and workflows
- Validate that proposed solutions meet business intent and operational realities
- Support change management and process alignment across teams
- Assist in identifying gaps and opportunities for improvement

### Goals
- Improve clarity of scope and requirements
- Reduce ambiguity in stakeholder expectations
- Support successful delivery of value-aligned outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria reviews and requirement sign-offs
- Business process and workflow documentation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Product Manager to prioritize and clarify business needs
- Provides requirements context and validation support to Developers and Project Managers
- Helps the Executive Sponsor understand business impact, trade-offs, and expected outcomes

---

## Risk Manager

### Role Summary
Risk Managers focus on identifying, evaluating, and mitigating project risks that could affect scope, schedule, budget, quality, or stakeholder confidence. They ensure risk issues are visible and addressed early.

### Responsibilities
- Identify risks, assumptions, dependencies, and constraints across the project
- Assess impact, likelihood, and ownership for unresolved risks
- Define mitigation, contingency, and escalation plans
- Monitor risk trends and flag emerging issues for stakeholders
- Facilitate risk reviews with project leadership and contributors

### Goals
- Minimize disruption from avoidable surprises
- Improve decision quality through risk-informed planning
- Maintain confidence in project execution and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers, issue tracking, and mitigation reviews
- Escalation briefs for leadership and decision-makers
- Regular status updates during planning and execution checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Project Manager to track project-level risk exposure and mitigation actions
- Works with the Executive Sponsor on material risks and strategic escalations
- Provides technical and operational risk input to Developers and Business Analysts when initiatives depend on process or delivery constraints

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives represent the interests of affected groups, partners, or business functions. They help ensure that outcomes reflect real user and stakeholder needs and that communication remains transparent throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder needs, concerns, and priorities
- Provide feedback on scope, timing, and user impact
- Help align cross-functional teams on expectations and decisions
- Support adoption, communication, and change readiness for affected groups
- Escalate concerns that could affect value realization or trust

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder perspectives are heard and acted on
- Improve clarity, trust, and buy-in across impacted groups
- Support smoother adoption and stakeholder satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder check-ins, feedback sessions, and readiness reviews
- Status updates and change communications
- Coordination with business and delivery leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides input to the Product Manager and Business Analyst on requirements and value expectations
- Recommends communication needs and feedback loops to the Project Manager
- Helps the Executive Sponsor understand stakeholder sentiment and organizational impact

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee testing strategy, quality standards, and release readiness. They help the organization deliver reliable work with clear validation evidence and predictable quality outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, quality gates, and release criteria
- Review quality risks in requirements, implementation, and deployment readiness
- Coordinate test planning, validation, and defect triage
- Support continuous improvement of quality processes and standards
- Confirm that outcomes meet readiness and acceptance expectations

### Goals
- Reduce defects and rework before release
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and readiness
- Support clear and consistent quality standards across teams

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, quality reviews, and defect triage discussions
- Release readiness checks and validation status updates
- Escalation of critical quality risks and blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to define testing coverage and validation approaches
- Works with the Project Manager and Executive Sponsor on release readiness and quality risk escalation
- Supports the Business Analyst and Product Manager by confirming that delivered outcomes satisfy acceptance criteria and stakeholder expectations
